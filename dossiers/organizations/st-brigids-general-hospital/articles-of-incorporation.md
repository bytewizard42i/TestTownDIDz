# Articles of Incorporation — St. Brigid's General Hospital

**Commonwealth filing PA-C-0048291 · filed April 12, 1987**

ARTICLE I. The name of the corporation is **St. Brigid's General Hospital**.

ARTICLE II. The purpose of the corporation is the operation of a general
acute-care hospital serving TestTown and surrounding counties, including
maternity services and the issuance of certificates of live birth to the
county records office.

ARTICLE III. Registered office: 1 Mercy Way, TestTown, PA 17801.

ARTICLE IV. Incorporators: Maren Okafor, Luis Reyes.
